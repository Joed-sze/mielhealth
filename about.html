About Aadon Trading – Your Trusted Bee Products & Beeswax Candle Supplier Since 2004

HANGZHOU AADON TRADING CO., LTD. is a specialized exporter of natural bee products and beeswax candles, headquartered in Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, China. Founded in 2004, we have built a reputation over two decades for reliable quality, consistent supply, and transparent service to buyers worldwide.

Our product portfolio covers the full range of bee product raw materials — including bulk honey, fresh royal jelly, royal jelly lyophilized powder, propolis extract, bee pollen, and refined beeswax — along with a dedicated beeswax candle line serving Jewish, religious, and premium home decor markets.

Our Story: Two Decades of Bee Product Expertise

What began in 2004 as a small trading company focused on royal jelly and honey has grown into a comprehensive bee product supplier serving supplement manufacturers, food processors, cosmetic formulators, and candle distributors across Asia, Europe, North America, and the Middle East. Our growth has been driven not by chasing the lowest price, but by building long-term partnerships based on quality consistency and honest communication.

We work directly with beekeeping cooperatives and certified processing facilities across China's major production regions — from the acacia forests of Sichuan to the linden groves of Northeast China and the rapeseed fields of the Yangtze River basin. This direct sourcing model allows us to control quality from the hive onward while offering competitive pricing.

Quality Assurance: From Hive to Shipping Container

Quality is the foundation of everything we do. Every batch of bee products we ship undergoes rigorous testing at multiple stages — upon receipt from the apiary, during processing, and before final packaging. Our partner factories are equipped with modern testing laboratories and hold internationally recognized certifications.

Certifications & Standards

  • HACCP — Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points certified facilities
  • ISO 9001 — Quality management systems
  • KOSHER — Kosher certification for select product lines
  • HALAL — Halal certification available for Middle Eastern markets
  • EU Organic — Organic certification for honey and royal jelly (EU standard)
  • NOP / USDA Organic — US National Organic Program compliant products

We provide a complete Certificate of Analysis (COA) for every shipment, covering moisture content, enzyme activity, HMF levels, heavy metal screening, and antibiotic residue testing. For first-time buyers, we always recommend independent third-party verification — we work with major labs including SGS, Intertek, and BV to arrange pre-shipment inspection upon request.

Why Global Buyers Choose Aadon Trading

1. Direct Sourcing, Transparent Pricing

We work directly with beekeeping cooperatives and processing facilities, cutting out unnecessary middlemen. This means you get factory-direct pricing while still having a single, accountable point of contact for your entire order.

2. Strategic Location Near Major Ports

Our headquarters in Hangzhou is within easy reach of both Shanghai Port and Ningbo-Zhoushan Port — two of the world's busiest container ports. This gives us flexible shipping options, competitive freight rates, and fast container turnaround times.

3. Wide Product Range, Single Point of Contact

Whether you need bulk honey, royal jelly, propolis, bee pollen, beeswax, or wholesale beeswax candles, you work with one dedicated account manager. No juggling multiple suppliers, no communication gaps, no finger-pointing when issues arise.

4. Flexible Order Sizes

We support buyers of all sizes — from first-time importers testing a market with sample orders to established brands placing full-container annual contracts. Our MOQ varies by product but is generally designed to be accessible for growing businesses.

5. Full Documentation Support

Every shipment includes a complete documentation package: Certificate of Origin, Health/Sanitary Certificate, COA, commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading. We also assist with destination-specific requirements such as FDA Prior Notice, EU TRACES registration, and Halal certification.
